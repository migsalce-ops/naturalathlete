01 · The Founder

Built by an
athlete.

Natural Athlete didn't start in a boardroom. It started in a gym, in a ring, on a track, on a field. Wherever the work was getting done.

The story

Natural Athlete was founded by Edryan Albright — a Cebu-based multi-sport athlete whose competitive life refused to live in one lane. Boxing rings. Flag football fields. Running routes. Weight rooms. Every discipline added a layer; every layer revealed the same truth: you don't become an athlete in a day. You become one by stacking them.

That phrase, Keep Stacking Days, isn't marketing. It's the rule he trained by, before it ever became a tagline. It's the answer to every question an athlete asks themselves on the days they don't feel like showing up: stack it anyway. The day counts. The streak counts. The compound counts.

Natural Athlete exists to put that rule on a hanger. Performance apparel built for the athlete who trains across sports, not the one who specializes in one. Tanks cut for the ring and the road. Tees that breathe under pads and under sun. Prints that look like the work feels: alive, in motion, unfinished.

Edryan has competed in boxing, flag football, and distance running for over a decade on Cebu's multi-sport circuit. Raised on the island's pre-dawn roads and weekend football fields, he trained in environments where athletes cross-trained out of necessity — not trend. Natural Athlete was founded in 2024 as a direct answer to that reality: performance apparel that earns its place in any athlete's bag, no matter which sport shows up on the schedule.

02 · The Mission

Built in Cebu.
Made for movement.

Natural Athlete is a Philippine-rooted athletic apparel label built around one promise: gear that earns its place in your bag, your gym, your routine. We design for athletes whose calendar isn't a single season. Boxers who run, runners who lift, lifters who play. Multi-sport, multi-day, every day.

Every drop is small. Every piece is field-tested. Every print and pattern is drawn from the brand's roots: roses, anarchy-A, hand-finished detail. Because the athletes wearing it don't move like everyone else, and the apparel shouldn't either.
